Accessibility Statement

Last updated: 28 October 2026

In short

  • We adapted the site for all visitors, including people with disabilities, children and adults.
  • There is an accessibility menu with text size, high contrast, night mode, 23 colour themes (including one for colour blindness), stopping animations, and reading the page aloud.
  • The site was tested with automated tools, but has not undergone a manual review by an external certified accessibility auditor.
  • Found a problem? Write to us and we will fix it as soon as possible.

Our commitment

We believe every child should be able to reach Help Site and use it, regardless of their vision, hearing, movement or attention abilities. We invested in making the site accessible from the planning stage, not as an afterthought.

Level of accessibility and the features used

The site was built, as far as possible, following the principles of the international Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG), level AA. Accessibility features include:

  • Accessibility menu (the round button in the corner of the screen): text size (90% to 160%), high contrast, stopping animations, a simpler font, underlined links, wider text spacing, grayscale, night mode, and reading the page aloud. Night mode is switched on manually, does not follow the device setting, and turns off when high contrast is switched on.
  • Colour themes: a choice of 23 colour themes in the accessibility menu, including a blue-and-orange theme designed for people with colour blindness. Every theme is also available in night mode.
  • Keyboard navigation for every part of the site, including the chat and the accessibility menu, with a clear focus indicator.
  • Screen reader compatibility: semantic HTML tags, alternative text for icons, and a "skip to main content" link.
  • Color contrast checked against the standard across all of the site's colour themes, in day and night modes. The check covered the main text and button colours and is not a substitute for a full accessibility audit.
  • Respecting the "reduce motion" setting (prefers-reduced-motion) of the operating system or browser.
  • Support for three languages (Hebrew, English and Arabic), with the correct text direction for each.

What has not been tested yet

The site was tested with automated tools and internal manual checks, but it has not yet undergone a comprehensive manual accessibility review by an external certified assessor. There may be components or situations we have not identified. We work to fix every issue that is reported to us.

Reporting an accessibility problem

Did you encounter a part of the site that is not accessible to you, or do you have a suggestion for improvement? We would love to hear: email oriyalic10@gmail.com or WhatsApp 077-321-1682. Where possible, please describe the problem, and the browser and device you encountered it on, so we can reproduce and fix it.
