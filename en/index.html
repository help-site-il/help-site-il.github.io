What's new

Every one of these is already working on the site — not just promises.

🛠️

Technical support in the chat

A problem with the site? You can talk to a technical volunteer when one is online. Open the chat, choose "Technical support" and then a topic. No sign-up and no personal details, and the chat is deleted when it ends. Also: the FAQ is now split into categories.
💬

Live chat with a volunteer

You can talk to a real volunteer in real time. Choose a topic in the chat, then "Talk to a real volunteer". No sign-up and no personal details, and the chat is deleted when it ends. While you wait you can use the notebook or a breathing exercise.
📓

A private notebook

A place to write for yourself before you reach out, with formatting, colors, tables and drawing. It is in the chat, next to the calming tools. What you write isn't saved, and it disappears when you close the page. It is sent to us only if you choose.
🌬️

Calming tools in the chat

Slow breathing and a 5-4-3-2-1 exercise, right inside the chat window. You can try them before you write to us, or while you wait for a reply.
📲

Works without internet

After one visit, the site opens even without internet, emergency numbers included, and you can add it to your home screen. When you are online, it updates itself.
🌍

Opens in your language

The site is available in Hebrew, English and Arabic. It opens in your browser’s language and remembers the one you choose for next time.

Got Questions?

About the site

Help Site is a free support and listening space for children and teens, run by volunteers. You can reach us by email or WhatsApp, and the site is available in Hebrew, English and Arabic.

Yes! Help Site is completely free for every child. We believe every child who needs help should get it, at no cost.

A live chat with a volunteer is available whenever volunteers are online. There are no fixed hours, and if nobody is online you can wait or write to us. By email and WhatsApp we usually try to reply within one business day (Sunday to Thursday). On Saturdays and holidays a reply may take longer.

Our volunteers are people who chose to listen to you, and they respect every child.

The site is available in Hebrew, English and Arabic. You can change language with the 🌐 button at the top of the page. The site opens in your browser's language and remembers the language you chose.

Privacy and safety

We protect your privacy and will not share what you write with anyone. The exceptions are a real danger to someone, or a legal duty, as set out in the Privacy policy.

Your conversations are private. We won't contact parents unless there is a genuine risk to your safety.

You can write to us at any hour by email or WhatsApp and we'll reply as soon as we can, but not always right away. If it's urgent or you are in danger, contact ERAN at 1201 (24 hours) or the police at 100 now.

No. The site is not an emergency service and is not a substitute for professional care. In an emergency or immediate danger, contact the emergency lines. The details are in the Terms of use.

In the live chat you start a conversation with a volunteer, with no sign-up, and the chat is deleted when it ends. By email or WhatsApp you write to us and we get back to you as soon as we can, within one business day. Note: on WhatsApp we can see your phone number, and by email we can see your email address. You can stop at any time.

No. In the live chat you do not need a name. By email or WhatsApp a nickname is enough. We recommend not writing your full name, address, school or phone number.

Yes. Quite a few people feel shy at first, and that is okay. You can write just a few words. You can also start with a calming exercise or the private notebook, without writing to anyone.

Technical support

First, refresh the page. If that does not help, open the site in a private window (incognito) or in another browser, and check that you are connected to the internet. If it still does not load, you can write to us by email or WhatsApp with the word "problem", and we will get back to you.

Some devices, networks and schools use filtering that blocks websites, and we cannot remove it from here. You can try another device or network, or ask a parent or a responsible adult to help. If you are in distress right now, you can call ERAN at 1201 (24 hours).

Yes, this is on purpose. The notebook does not save anything, and what you wrote is deleted when you close the page. If you want to keep something, you can copy it first. Sending it to us is only if you choose to.

After one visit, the site also opens without internet, including the emergency numbers, and you can add it to your home screen. The live chat, email and WhatsApp need an internet connection.

In the chat, choose "Technical support" and then a topic. If a technical volunteer is online, you can talk to them in a live chat. If not, you can write to us by email or WhatsApp with the word "problem", and we will usually reply within one business day (Sunday to Thursday).
