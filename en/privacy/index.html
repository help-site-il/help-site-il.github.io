Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

In short

  • You can use the site without registering or identifying yourself. There are no accounts and no passwords.
  • The site does not use cookies or ads, and does not track individual people. It only counts visits anonymously (see below).
  • If you contact us by email or WhatsApp, we receive what you write, and we use it only to reply to you.
  • We do not sell your information and we do not pass it on. The exceptions are a real danger to someone, or a legal duty (see below).
  • A first name or nickname is enough. There is no need for a full name, an address, or details that are not needed.

Who we are

Help Site is a support and listening space for children and teens. It is run as a volunteer initiative by Oriya Lichtenberg (“we”). For questions and requests about privacy: helpsite.support@gmail.com, or on WhatsApp 077-321-1682.

What information is collected

  • When you browse: we do not collect personal information while you browse. The site has no registration, accounts, or forms. As with any website, the hosting provider that serves the pages may record technical connection data (such as IP address, browser type, and time of visit) for operation and security. We do not use it to identify visitors.
  • Contacting us by email: when you press “Send a message by email”, your device’s email app opens with a ready-made message. Information reaches us only if you send that message, and includes your email address and what you wrote, for example a nickname, a way to reach you, and the topic. We ask you to share only what is needed so that we can get back to you.
  • Contacting us on WhatsApp: when you press “Send a message on WhatsApp” or start a chat, we see your phone number, the profile name and picture you have set, and the content of the chat. WhatsApp is operated by Meta and is subject to its privacy policy.
  • The topic buttons on the site: choosing a topic (for example “Sadness” or “School”) happens in your browser only. The choice is not sent to us and is not stored.
  • Display preferences: settings in the accessibility menu (text size, contrast, night mode, colors, and more) are saved only in your browser, so you do not have to choose them again. They are not sent to us. You can reset them at any time from the accessibility menu or by clearing the site’s data in your browser.
  • Site language: the site opens in your browser’s language (Hebrew, English or Arabic). The language that was chosen, automatically or by you in the language menu, is saved only in your browser, so the site opens in the same language next time. It is not sent to us. You can delete it at any time by clearing the site’s data in your browser.
  • Use without internet: so the site can open even when there is no internet, your browser keeps a copy of the site’s pages on your device (using a component called a service worker). The copy stays on your device, is not sent to us, and contains no personal information. When you are online, the site updates itself. You can delete the copy at any time by clearing the site’s data in your browser.
  • Anonymous visit counting: to know roughly how many people visit the site and which pages are read, the site uses a counting service called GoatCounter. According to its privacy policy, it uses no cookies, does not store IP addresses, and does not store information in your browser. Only aggregate data is kept, such as the number of visits per page, browser and operating system type, country, and the site you came from. To avoid counting the same visit twice, temporary information is kept in server memory only, for up to 8 hours. The counter does not see what you write in the chat, and it does not run on the volunteer pages. The script is loaded from the domain gc.zgo.at, and the data is stored on servers in Europe (Finland and Germany).
  • Live chat with a volunteer (only if you start it): Only after you press “Start chat” does the page contact Google servers (Firebase) to run the chat. A temporary anonymous ID is created, with no name, no email and no cookies, and it is kept in the page memory only. The messages are stored on the servers until the chat ends. Google may see an IP address, like on any website; volunteers cannot see it. The volunteer sees what you write in the chat and the language of the page. In the technical support chat, if the volunteer asks, you can attach a screenshot of the problem, and the volunteer sees it. Please crop or hide anything that is not the problem, and do not capture names, messages, notifications or people. The picture is kept on the servers until the chat ends and is deleted together with the messages. Volunteers are not professionals, and the chat is not an emergency service.

Why we use information

  • To reply to your message and to offer a listening ear and first support.
  • To tailor our reply, and to refer you to a suitable organization when needed.
  • To protect the safety of the person who contacted us, when necessary.

We do not use information for advertising, marketing, or building profiles, and we do not sell it.

Who we share it with

  • The team that handles messages: only the site’s volunteers who handle messages have access to them.
  • Technical services: the email service where we receive messages (Google Gmail), WhatsApp (Meta), the site’s hosting provider, and the anonymous visit-counting service GoatCounter. Some of them operate outside Israel and are subject to their own privacy policies.
  • Exceptional cases: if a message raises a real concern that you or someone else may be harmed, we may contact parents, welfare services, emergency services, or the authorities in order to protect safety. We will do the same if we are required to by law or by a court order. We will act only to the extent needed, and where possible we will let you know.

How long information is kept, and how to delete it

We keep messages only for as long as needed to deal with them. You can ask us at any time to delete your message and the information in it, and we will handle the request promptly. Please note that deleting on our side does not delete messages kept by the email and WhatsApp providers or on your own device.

A live chat is deleted from the servers when it ends. If the browser is closed in the middle, an automatic deletion mechanism usually removes it within about a day. We do not keep the content of chats.

Children and teens

The site is also meant for children and teens. We do not require you to share details in order to use the site, and we do not ask for information that is not needed. If you are under 18, a first name or nickname is enough, and there is no need to share an address, a school name, or other identifying details. When you can, we recommend also involving an adult you trust. Parents and guardians may contact us on behalf of their child to ask to see or delete information.

Information security

We take reasonable measures to protect information, and the site is served over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). Still, no method guarantees complete security, so please do not send us passwords, credit card details, or sensitive information that is not needed for your message.

Cookies and local storage

The site does not use cookies and includes no advertising or tools that track individual people. It does include an anonymous visit counter (GoatCounter, see above), which does not store cookies or any other information in your browser. The fonts are hosted on the site itself and are not loaded from external services. Your browser’s local storage is used only for the display preferences, the site language, and a saved copy of the site’s pages that lets them open without internet, as described above. Links to external services, such as WhatsApp, take you to them, and they are subject to their own policies.

Your rights

Under Israel’s Privacy Protection Law, 5741-1981, you may ask to see the information kept about you, to correct it, or to delete it. Contact us using any of the details below and we will try to reply promptly.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version will appear here, with the date of the update.

Contact

For questions and requests about privacy: email helpsite.support@gmail.com or WhatsApp 077-321-1682. Information about the terms of using the site is in the Terms of use.
