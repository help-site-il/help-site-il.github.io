Terms of Use

Last updated: 24 September 2026

In short

  • The site is a place to listen and support. It is not a substitute for professional care, whether mental, medical, or other.
  • The site is not an emergency service. In an emergency or immediate danger, contact the emergency lines (details below).
  • Those who reply to you are trained volunteers, not professionals. We do not promise a response time.
  • We protect your privacy, except in the case of a real danger (see the Privacy policy).
  • Please be respectful and do not harm others.

What the site and service are

Help Site is a free support and listening space for children and teens. Replies are given by a team of volunteers who have been trained to offer a listening ear and first support. Using the site means you agree to these terms.

What the site is not

  • The site is not mental, medical, legal, or any other professional treatment, and it is not a diagnosis or professional advice.
  • The volunteers do not replace professionals, and we do not promise any particular result.
  • The site is not an emergency service and does not operate around the clock.

In an emergency or danger

If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call now (these numbers work inside Israel):

  • Police: 100
  • ERAN, emotional first aid, 24 hours a day: 1201
  • Hotline 105, protection of children and teens online, 24 hours a day: 105
  • Hotline 118 of the Ministry of Welfare, information and guidance in emergencies: 118

Opening hours and replies

Replies are given during the opening hours listed on the home page. Outside these hours you can leave a message, and we will get back to you as soon as possible during opening hours. We do not promise a specific response time.

Confidentiality and privacy

We protect your privacy. The exception is a case where a real concern arises that you or someone else may be harmed, or where we are required to act by law. In that case we will act to protect safety, which may include contacting parents, welfare services, or the authorities. Full details are in the Privacy policy.

Fair use

  • You do not have to share any details in order to use the site. If you choose to share details, please share accurate and minimal details.
  • Do not impersonate others, harass, threaten, or send offensive content, and do not use the site for advertising.
  • Do not try to disrupt the site or break into it.
  • We may end a conversation that does not follow these rules.

Site content

The site’s content was created for the site. You may share the link to it. Do not copy or distribute the content for commercial purposes without our permission.

Links and external services

The site links to third-party services, such as WhatsApp, email, and emergency lines. These services are not our responsibility and are subject to their own terms and policies.

Accessibility

We have invested in making the site accessible, with an accessibility menu that includes text size, contrast, stopping animations, reading the page aloud, and more. Did you run into difficulty? Write to us and we will deal with it.

Limitation of liability

The site and the information in it are provided as they are, and we make a reasonable effort to provide them properly. We are not responsible for technical faults or temporary interruptions in service, including faults in third-party services that the site relies on. Nothing here limits the rights you have under law.

Changes and governing law

We may update these terms from time to time. The current version will appear here, with the date of the update. These terms are governed by Israeli law.
