What's new

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Everything added to the site in the past month. Older updates drop off the list by themselves.

  • 9 November 2026

    Technical support in the chat

    A problem with the site? You can talk to a technical volunteer when one is online. Open the chat, choose "Technical support" and then a topic. No sign-up and no personal details, and the chat is deleted when it ends. Also: the FAQ is now split into categories.

    Open the chat →

  • 1 November 2026

    Live chat with a volunteer

    You can talk to a real volunteer in real time. Choose a topic in the chat, then "Talk to a real volunteer". No sign-up and no personal details, and the chat is deleted when it ends. While you wait you can use the notebook or a breathing exercise.

    Open the chat →

  • 31 October 2026

    A private notebook

    A place to write for yourself before you reach out, with formatting, colors, tables and drawing. It is in the chat, next to the calming tools. What you write isn't saved, and it disappears when you close the page. It is sent to us only if you choose.

    Open the notebook →

  • 29 October 2026

    Calming tools in the chat

    Slow breathing and a 5-4-3-2-1 exercise, right inside the chat window. You can try them before you write to us, or while you wait for a reply.

    Open the chat →

  • 29 October 2026

    Works without internet

    After one visit, the site opens even without internet, emergency numbers included. To open it offline, use the icon on your home screen, a bookmark, or type the address, because a Google search needs internet. When you are online, the site updates itself.

    Go to the home page →

  • 29 October 2026

    Opens in your language

    The site opens in your browser’s language (Hebrew, English or Arabic) and remembers the one you choose for next time. The language is saved only in your browser.

    What is saved in your browser →

  • 28 October 2026

    Night mode and 23 colour themes

    A night mode that is easy on the eyes, and a choice of 23 colour palettes, including one made for colour blindness.

    Open the accessibility menu →

No updates in the past month.
